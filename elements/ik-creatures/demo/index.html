Procedural IK creatures

Every leg is a two-bone chain solved each step: feet plant on the ground and stay put, and a leg only lifts when it is stretched and its neighbours are down. The body rides on its feet, follows its path and turns its head to the firefly. Click or tap to move the firefly.
