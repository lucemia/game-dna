Card illustrations

Painted 2:3 card art generated once (one style lock, light from the top-left, a calm lower half for text) and a card frame drawn in code: title, cost, type and rules text are live text, never part of the image. The same art fills the G-07 upgrade-draft cards.

In the upgrade draft (G-07)

The draft module is unchanged: art.drawDraft(ctx, draft) replaces draft.draw(ctx) and paints each card's illustration under its chip, name and text. Arrows / A D choose, Z take, R reroll, X skip, or click.

Card frame renderer
