Squash, stretch & anticipation

Every character here is one still image. The module bends it about the feet: a crouch before a jump or a swing, a stretch while rising and falling, a squash on landing and on taking a hit, a springy overshoot back, a lean into movement and a slow breath at rest. Area is kept (sx×sy = 1), and the game’s hit boxes never change. Left: the same game without the module.
