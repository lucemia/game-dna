Motion-input special moves

An input buffer reads quarter circles, the forward-down-diagonal motion, charge and a full turn, with leniency windows; the display shows the last inputs and names each special. The demo plays a recorded tape; press any key or a pad button to play yourself.

Move list

Arrows are drawn for the way the hero faces now: they mirror when the hero crosses over.

Controls

← → walk · ↓ crouch · ↑ jump · J/X A slash · K/C B spell · pad: d-pad or stick, face buttons
