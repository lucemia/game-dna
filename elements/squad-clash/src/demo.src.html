Auto-resolved squad clash with forecast

Two 2×3 squads meet. Each unit acts by its own “condition → action” rules, in speed order; guards step in for weak allies, allies follow up. The forecast is the same seeded simulation run ahead, so the clash you commit plays out exactly as shown.

Try: click two of your slots to swap them, click a unit twice to change its tactics, and watch the striped part of each HP bar (what the forecast says it will lose) move. Then commit.
