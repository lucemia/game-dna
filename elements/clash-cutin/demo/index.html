A-04

Side-view clash cut-in

When an attack is confirmed on the map, the game cuts to a side-view scene: the attacker leaps in and strikes, hits land with a freeze frame and a draining HP bar, misses are dodged, criticals flash, the counter follows and a fallen unit fades. The numbers are the game's own; the scene only stages them. Z skips, X toggles x2.

Scripted tour: press a key or a button to take over.Arrows cursor · Z select / attack / skip · X back / x2
