Fog of war

Tiles outside your units' sight sink under a drifting, dithered fog with a soft one-tile edge. Hidden enemies are not drawn, the spot where you last saw one keeps a faint silhouette, and a "!" pops when one comes into view. Forests block sight, hills extend it.
