Branching choices with flags

A choice menu after a line: 2 to 4 options, a cursor, disabled options that say why, an optional countdown that picks a default. Picks set flags (gold, met, plan) that later lines check, jump to labels and rejoin; once-only lines and options are remembered for saves. Drives the H-01 dialogue box.
