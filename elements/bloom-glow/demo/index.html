Bloom & glow

Bright pixels and whatever the game draws into an emissive layer bleed soft light: soft-knee threshold, a dual-filter blur chain at 1/2 to 1/16 resolution, tinted additive or screen composite, and a short flash of bloom on big hits. The HUD stays crisp. Runs on the I-08 WebGL pipeline; without WebGL the game looks exactly as before.
