Scripted cutscenes

A small script plays a scene in the game's own world: characters walk with real steps and face where they go, the camera pans and cuts, lines type out with a speaker plate, emotes pop over heads, letterbox bars frame it all. Hold X to skip: it fades to black and lands on exactly the state the scene would have ended in. Control comes back once.

Arrow keys take over the traveller (walk into the square or up to the captain to start a scene) · Z / tap: next line · hold X / long-press: skip. Every frame is a function of the clock: ?t=<ms>&freeze=1 gives the same still every time, ?look=pixel the pixel interface.
