Upgrade draft · pick 1 of 3

At a moment the game chooses (level-up, chest, wave clear) the world pauses and three upgrade cards deal in, flip and glow by rarity. Move between them and take one; cards that share a tag with your build show a synergy hint; reroll or skip if allowed. The pick flies into your build strip. Upgrades are a plain table the designer edits; draws are seeded, weighted by rarity and never offer a maxed upgrade.
