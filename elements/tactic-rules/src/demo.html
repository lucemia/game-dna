Conditional tactic rules

Every unit decides with an ordered list of rules, "condition → action": the first rule that holds and whose action is usable wins, otherwise the default. The evaluator is pure and explains itself (which rule fired and why the ones above it did not); players edit the list in a compact editor, and a plate in battle says why each unit did what it did.

Watch: every action comes from a rule; the plate names the unit, the rule and the target. At 9.5 s the editor opens on Sel, lowers "3+ foes → Volley" to 2+ and moves it to the top; her next turn casts Volley.T edit rules · Space pause

Decision log
