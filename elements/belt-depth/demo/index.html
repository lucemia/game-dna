Belt depth

Fighters walk left / right and up / down the street. A swing only connects with someone on the same lane (within the depth tolerance); jumps lift the body while the shadow stays on the cobbles; whoever stands nearer the front is drawn in front. The demo plays a recorded brawl; press any key, or use the buttons under the stage, to fight yourself.

Controls

← → walk · ↑ ↓ step into / out of the street · J/Z strike · K/X jump (strike in the air to drop on a foe)

Depth

The street is 40 px deep. With a tolerance of 7 a strike connects when the two fighters stand at most 7 px apart in depth; turn the lane cue on to see the striker's lane drawn on the cobbles while the blow is live.
