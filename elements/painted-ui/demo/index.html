The icon sheet: 24 icons, one light, one scale

One generation of a 6×4 sheet, split by shape (not by a blind grid), trimmed, scaled so every icon's longer side is 84 % of its cell and centred by its bounds. Shown in the kit's slots: the full sheet at 36 px, then rows at 28, 44 and 64 px. On a narrow screen this panel scrolls sideways.

Existing elements pick it up: G-01 shop and C-01 menus through the skin

The unchanged shop (G-01) and window-cursor (C-01) modules on a non-pixel skin. Right: after one call, ui.attach(GameDNA_Skin, {skins: [skinId]}), which registers the frames for the skin's frame roles and the icons for the icon role (SPEC v0.5 bitmap roles). Pixel skins ignore it by design.
