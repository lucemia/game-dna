Time rewind

Hold R and the world runs backwards: every step the game's state is copied into a ring buffer, and while the key is held older copies come back, up to 3× as fast. Let go and play continues from that moment, with what you now know. The bolt that hit the swordsman flies back into the imp; on the second take he raises his shield. Characters keep facing where they faced: walking backwards in time is not turning around.

take 1 world 0.00 s history 0.0 s memory 0 KB

R hold to rewind · ←→ walk · Z jump · X sword · C shield · P pause (nothing is recorded while paused). A move key takes over from the script; “Script” gives it back.

The module copies the whole game state once per fixed step (here about 5 KB, roughly 280 KB per second) and keeps the last History seconds. Facing, animation phase and every timer are part of that state, so a rewound frame is exactly the frame that was played; ?t=&freeze=1 stills repeat byte for byte.
