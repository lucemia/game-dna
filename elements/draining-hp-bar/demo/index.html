Draining HP bar

Damage drops the fill at once while a pale loss segment holds where the health was, then drains down; quick hits extend the hold and merge into one segment. A heal shows a gain segment first, then the fill rises to meet it. Low health pulses. One module draws the boss bar, the party HUD bars and the small bars over units, smooth on HD and crisp on pixel canvases.
