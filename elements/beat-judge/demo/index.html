Beat judge

A beat clock and an input judge: every press is rated Perfect, Good or Miss against the beat, measured at the moment of the key or tap event, minus audio latency and the player's calibration offset. Beats follow the chip-music battle loop (or a click track); the game decides what a rating does. Here a Perfect strike deals double damage and a miss lets the brute counter.

Sound off: click anywhere to turn it on
