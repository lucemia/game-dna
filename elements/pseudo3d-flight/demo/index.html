Pseudo-3D flight ground (B-06)

The top-down tile map below the hero becomes a perspective, scanline-scaled ground with a horizon sky band. Take off and the view zooms and tilts into flight; turn to bank (the horizon tilts slightly), speed up, slow down; land only on flat ground. Billboards scale with distance; the mount casts a shadow; the world wraps.

Parameters

Walking: arrows move, X take off. Flying: ←→ turn and bank, ↑ faster, ↓ slower, Z land (green corners = flat ground). Any key stops the scripted tour. URL: ?t=ms&freeze=1 for a deterministic frame, ?speed= ?sky=dusk … for any parameter.
