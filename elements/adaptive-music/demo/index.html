Adaptive Music

The game only reports its state (exploring, fighting, a boss, low health). The music answers with layers over one chip track: drums come in for a fight, a tense pulse line for low health, the boss gets its own track. Every change waits until the state has held for a moment, lands on the next bar line, and never overlaps the previous one.
