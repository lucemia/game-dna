Keyword Tooltips

Rich text whose keywords open tooltips: hover with a mouse, long-press or tap on touch, Tab through them with the keyboard. A keyword inside a tooltip opens a second one; click, tap or T pins a tooltip. Tooltips stay on screen, never cover the word they explain, and glide instead of jumping.

Mouse: hover a coloured word, click to pin, move into a tooltip to open the next one, click outside to close · Keyboard: Tab / Shift+Tab focus, T pin and step inside, Esc close · Touch: long-press or tap a word, tap outside to close. Moving the mouse over the scene stops the tour.
