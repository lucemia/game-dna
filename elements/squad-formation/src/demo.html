Squad formation grid

Every side fights as a 2×3 squad: a front row that faces the enemy and a back row behind it. Melee reaches only the front row while anyone stands there; arrows and spells reach everyone and hit the back row harder; a melee fighter in the back swings at half power. Drag units (or use the arrows and Z) in the formation editor, press DEPLOY, then send the raiders at your line.
