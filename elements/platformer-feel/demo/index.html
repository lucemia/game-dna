Platformer movement feel (B-08)

Side-view movement against a tile map: acceleration curves, variable jump height, coyote time, jump buffering, apex hang, terminal fall, an 8-way dash that refills on landing, wall slide and wall jump, one-way planks, corner correction and sub-pixel positions drawn on whole pixels. The HUD shows the coyote and buffer windows and calls out each assist; Markers (O) switches to the lab view of the whole room with the jump arc, event markers and a live velocity graph. A bot runs the test room until you press a key.
