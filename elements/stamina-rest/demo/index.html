Stamina & outpost rest

Squads spend stamina on every march and every attack. At zero they are exhausted: they cannot attack (or move slower) until they recover. Resting inside a camp, outpost or shrine their side holds refills the pips; standing alone in an enemy or empty site claims it. Works on a turn clock (rest per phase) and on a real-time clock (rest per second).

Watch: pips drain on marches and attacks; a spent squad shows TIRED, its next attack is refused (TOO TIRED) and it falls back to camp, where REST +4 refills it with embers. Clock

Parameters

Costs (attack, march, regen, rest) follow the pace for the current clock. Switching the clock keeps your other choices and resets only those costs.

Deterministic: ?t=14000&freeze=1 shows the same frame every time. Parameters go in the URL, e.g. ?clock=realtime&rule=noAttack. Real-time: arrows / WASD move, Z swings (autoplay resumes after 4 s idle).
