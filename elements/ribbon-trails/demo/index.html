Ribbon trails

Smooth tapered ribbons behind a sword, a dash, a bolt or a drifting wisp: a curve through the last few positions, thinning and fading with age, with a bright additive core. A sword gives its hilt too, so the swing leaves a swept crescent. Blinks, scene changes and pauses never draw a line across the screen. Compare with Afterimages (the older look: sprite copies) under Style.

Everything advances in update(dt) from game time only, so ?t=&freeze=1 gives the same frame every run. update(0) (pause, hit-stop) freezes the ribbons in place; a jump much larger than the recent steps starts a new strip.
