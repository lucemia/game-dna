World-space bark bubbles

Short lines pop out above characters, right in the world: they type in, follow the speaker as it walks, keep clear of each other, other characters and the HUD, stay on screen with a pointer when the speaker walks off the edge, and fade by themselves. The game never pauses.

Same module on a native pixel canvas

384 × 216 native pixels, shown at a whole multiple. On a small canvas the module switches by itself to crisp pixel bubbles with its built-in 6 × 8 bitmap font: a 1 px ink outline with stair-step corners, a lit top row, a shaded bottom row, a stair-step tail and a 1 px drop shadow.
