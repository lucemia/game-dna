B-04

Isometric height & jump

A 2:1 isometric tactics map where every tile has a height. A unit climbs at most its jump, drops at most jump + fall, shoots farther from high ground, hits harder from above or behind, and picks a facing when it is done. Blocks in front hide units behind them; the hidden unit gets an outline. The camera turns in 90° steps.
