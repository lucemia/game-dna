Grappling hook & swing (B-16)

Fire a hook at iron rings or any rock face, hang on a real rope, swing like a pendulum, pump with left and right, reel in and out, and let go with the swing's momentum. The rope bends around corners and unbends on the way back. The game keeps its own collision: the module only proposes the swing's velocity.

←→ run, pump the swing · Z/Space jump (on the rope: jump off) · X/C fire the hook, hold to hang on, release to let go · ↑↓ aim steeper / flatter, on the rope reel in / out · click or tap the scene to fire at that spot · B bot tour
