Paper doll

A pixel character built from layers (body, hair, hat, top, legs, boots, weapon, shield) that you can swap and recolour. Every part is drawn on one shared skeleton, so any combination lines up in every frame of the four-direction walk and the attack. The weapon stays in the right hand when the character turns: facing left it is the far hand, behind the body.
