Sprite stacking

Pseudo-3D props from stacked pixel slices: every crate, tree, house and cart is a small voxel model drawn as its slices, one screen row above the other, so the camera can turn all the way round. The villagers stay flat sprites, and every frame they are sorted with the stacks by their ground footprints, so they walk behind the well and in front of the stall from any side.

 

0.0 s

Drag the scene or press Q / E to turn the camera; arrow keys walk the hero. Every frame is a function of the clock: ?t=<ms>&freeze=1 gives the same still at any window size.
