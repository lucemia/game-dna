Touch controls with edge-latched input

A pixel D-pad (or stick), A/B and START appear on phones below the game, in its side gutters or over its corners, never on the HUD. Every finger is tracked, presses sink and light up, and every input is edge-latched: a tap shorter than one game step still counts exactly once.
