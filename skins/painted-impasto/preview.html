Painted Impasto HD skin: painted-impasto

Hand-painted HD 2D in thick oil: broad impasto strokes with a lit ridge and a dark lip, a warm golden key light from the upper left, deep plum-brown shadows, bold umber outlines on every figure, and ornate framed UI in oxblood lacquer and hammered gold. Units, props, the HUD portrait, the ground and the window frames come from a small generated pack locked to one style sheet; everything else, and everything when the pack is missing, is painted in code with the same brush.
