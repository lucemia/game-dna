Same element, twelve styles

One tactics moment (seed 7) drawn by the real grid-range, damage-number and window modules through twelve skins. Only the skin changes: the reach, the path, where each number lands and when the menu opens are identical in every cell.

1.40 s

Deterministic: ?t=<ms>&freeze=1 renders one moment, ?only=<slug> shows one style large, ?focus=grid|numbers|window picks the element. The scene is scene/shared-scene.js; the contract is SPEC.md.

Map cells, units and mood come from each skin's tile, unitToken and atmosphere hooks; a skin without them gets the generic ones from skin-api.js.
