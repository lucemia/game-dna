# Prompt fragment: cel (Cel-shaded anime)

Art style: cel-shaded anime, original look. Smooth vector rendering (not pixel art). Every shape is flat saturated colour closed by a bold dark-violet ink outline (#1c1433, about 1 native px for UI, 1.6 px per side on numerals, never pure black). Shading is exactly one hard second tone per colour, cut by a straight or diagonal line: no gradients, no blur. Drop shadows are solid ink offsets.

UI: windows are slanted parallelogram panels (4 px slant) in saturated team colours (player blue #2a6bff, enemy red #ff3b56, neutral violet #4a3fb8) with a thick ink outline, a hard shade band along the bottom, a 1 px rim light on top and two diagonal yellow accent stripes in the top-right corner. Text is a heavy rounded sans, uppercase, white with ink outline and a hard drop; the lower half of each line can switch to a second tone. Bars are slanted, outlined, light-over-shade.

Numbers: big italic rounded comic numerals with ink outline, hard drop, a two-tone fill, a white glint and speed lines trailing left; critical hits burst out of a red-and-yellow starburst with radial ink lines and a slanted red CRITICAL ribbon. Range overlays are flat saturated tints with a bright inner edge and a thin ink rim. The move path is a chunky outlined yellow arrow with a triangular head and marching white dashes.

Motion is snappy: overshoot on pop-ins, squash on landing, a white flash on impact. Skin id: `cel`.
