# Prompt fragment: 1930s rubber-hose cartoon

Art style: 1930s rubber-hose cartoon, original art only (no existing cartoon characters, studios or names).

- Characters: bendy noodle limbs with no elbows or knees, white four-finger gloves, pie-cut eyes (black pupil with a wedge cut out), big round black shoes, big heads, squash-and-stretch bodies.
- Line: thick, EVEN ink outlines (never tapered) that "boil": redraw every outline with a slightly different wobble 12 times a second, three drawings in a loop.
- Palette: a sepia print ramp (ink `#16110c`, paper `#efe3c8`, midtone ground) plus only two two-strip accents, red-orange `#c4532f` and blue-green `#3f8a7e`, used for gameplay meaning only. Characters and gloves take the extremes; the ground stays mid grey-sepia. One hard shade crescent, no gradients or glow.
- Motion: the whole world breathes on a 120 BPM beat in phase (5 % stretch about the feet), drawings on twos, bouncy overshoot, airborne stretch and landing squash.
- UI: white-on-black title cards with a double white rule and dot-ring corners; hand-lettered fat caps; a white glove as the pointer.
- Numbers: fat white numerals with a thick ink outline and drop; criticals on a jagged POW burst with a ribbon label; MISS on a puff cloud.
- Ranges: flat tints plus hatching (move /, attack \, threat cross-hatch); the move path is a white hose ending in a pointing glove.
- Film pass (optional): grain, flicker, dust, vignette, rounded gate corners, slight gate weave.
