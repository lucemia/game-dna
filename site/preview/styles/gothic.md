# Prompt fragment: Dark gothic painterly

Art style: dark gothic painterly, original art only.

- Mood: a candle-lit crypt painted in oils. Most of the screen is near-black; light comes only from visible braziers, candles or lanterns as warm pools, with heavy chiaroscuro, a dark vignette and slow drifting fog.
- Palette: desaturated stone, iron and leather with two accents: blood red (`#8e1c1f`) for danger, foes and criticals, and old gold (`#c9a04a`) for UI, cursor and path. Text is bone white `#e6dcc4`; never pure black or white.
- Texture: procedural brush-stroke noise over windows, bars, numerals and ground.
- Windows: chamfered dark-iron rims around painted leather, an engraved gold inner line, gold filigree scrolls in all four corners, soft black shadow. Selected row is a blood-red band.
- Type: engraved serif capitals (Cinzel, Georgia fallback), gilded gradients for titles, soft dark halo.
- Numbers: struck-metal serif numerals (gold damage, blood-red criticals with an ember glow, pale green heal, steel-blue MP), dark outline, inner shadow, a smoke puff where they land.
- Grid overlays: smoky washes (pale blue move, blood red attack with ember edge, violet threat), glowing wobbly edges, slow breathing.
- Path: a thin gold line ending in a spearhead, with a travelling glint. Cursor: gold spear-tip corners; menu pointer: a small gold spearhead.
- Motion: slow and heavy, ease-in-out, no bouncy overshoot.
