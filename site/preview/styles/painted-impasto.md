**Art style: Painted impasto HD.** Hand-painted HD 2D in thick oil paint. No pixel grid, no flat vector fills.

- Canvas: render at device resolution (display px × devicePixelRatio), smoothing on.
- The brush: build surfaces from short broad strokes with a ragged bristle edge, a lighter ridge on the side facing the light and a darker lip on the other side. Use it for ground, range washes (horizontal bands per row), gauges, the move arrow and panels.
- Light: one warm golden key light from the upper left; deep cool plum-brown shadows toward the lower right; contact and short cast shadows down-right; warm pools under fires.
- Figures and props: a bold dark umber outline (`#1f130c`, about 1/40 of the height) around every character and object; sturdy proportions, about 4 heads tall.
- Palette: ochre gold, burnt sienna, deep crimson, slate blue, olive green, warm cream.
- UI: ornate frames: dark oxblood or umber lacquer panels with visible strokes, hammered-gold rims with leaf corners and a small team-coloured gem; the selected row is a gold paint swash. Alegreya SC for titles, Alegreya Sans for items.
- Numbers: heavy sans numerals with a thick umber outline, cream-to-colour fill with a lit streak; crits, heals and MP sit on a broad painted swash.
- Optional generated art: generate units, props, portraits, ground and frames from one locked style sheet and one style paragraph; store prompts and sha256; keep the code-drawn look as fallback.
- Original art only; English UI.
