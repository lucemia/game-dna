Beat judge

A beat judge that rates every press Perfect, Good or Miss, with a calibration for audio lag. An add-on for any game.

Live demoPress Turn sound on to hear the beat, then tap Z, Space or the arena on the beat.

What it does

A beat clock and an input judge for rhythm actions: every press the game accepts is rated Perfect, Good or Miss against the nearest beat, measured at the moment of the key or tap event (never the frame that handles it).

Made forturn-based RPG (timed attacks and parries on the beat)action and platformer (attacks or dashes that hit harder on the beat)rhythm and music games, rhythm-action hybridsany game with a music loop that should matter to play

Tested: 6/6 runs on unseen games passed · visual review 7.7
